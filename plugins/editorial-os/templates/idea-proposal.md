## Reader-value contract

- Reader: who specifically needs this piece?
- Reader's problem: the question, decision, misconception or task they have.
- Expected outcome: what they will understand or be able to do afterwards.
- Distinctive contribution: what this adds beyond existing ACRR pages and readily available sources.
- Evidence: sources, data, derivations, experiments or interviews that support the contribution.
- Limitations: what the piece will not establish or solve.

## Relationship to existing ACRR content

List the ACRR pages that cover the same ground (search the site and `eos inventory`), and say for each one whether this piece replaces, extends or links to it. A proposal that repeats an existing page without distinct value is rejected or becomes a revision of that page.
