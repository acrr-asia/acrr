This series is intended for readers who are comfortable with basic circuit analysis (Ohm's law, voltage dividers), calculus and elementary statistics. It starts with what a measurement is and follows the signal from the physical world to a number that can be trusted, in seven entries: measurement and sensor characteristics, sensing principles, signal conditioning, sampling and data conversion, noise and interference, smart sensors and microelectromechanical systems (MEMS), and finally calibration and the fusion of several sensors into one better estimate.
