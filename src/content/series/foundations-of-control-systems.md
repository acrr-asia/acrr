  - "Relate the eigenvalues of a system matrix to stability and transient behavior."
  - "Recognize the assumptions and limits of a model before designing a controller around it."

This series is intended for readers who are comfortable with calculus and basic linear algebra. Each entry introduces one idea, states the assumptions it relies on, and points to the next idea in the sequence. The five entries go from the closed loop and its performance measures, through proportional feedback on a first-order system and state-space models, to stability from eigenvalues and the design of a state-feedback controller.
