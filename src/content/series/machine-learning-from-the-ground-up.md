This series is written in Vietnamese first. Its entries are published in Vietnamese as they are completed, at [/vi/series/machine-learning-from-the-ground-up](/vi/series/machine-learning-from-the-ground-up); the English versions listed below are planned.

The thirty entries are grouped into eight parts. Part I (entries 1 to 4) reviews linear algebra, matrix calculus and probability, and introduces maximum likelihood and maximum a posteriori estimation. Part II (entries 5 to 8) sets out the basic concepts of machine learning, feature engineering, linear regression and overfitting. Part III (entries 9 to 11) covers three simple methods: k-nearest neighbors, k-means clustering and naive Bayes classifiers. Part IV (entries 12 to 16) moves from gradient descent through the perceptron, logistic regression and softmax regression to multilayer neural networks and backpropagation. Part V (entries 17 to 19) builds recommender systems. Part VI (entries 20 to 22) treats dimensionality reduction with the singular value decomposition, principal component analysis and linear discriminant analysis. Part VII (entries 23 to 25) introduces convex sets, convex functions, convex optimization problems and Lagrange duality, and Part VIII (entries 26 to 29) applies them to support vector machines. Entry 30 covers the method of Lagrange multipliers that several earlier entries use.

The outline follows the structure of the Vietnamese textbook *Machine Learning cơ bản* by Vũ Hữu Tiệp; the text, examples and code of this series are written for ACRR.
